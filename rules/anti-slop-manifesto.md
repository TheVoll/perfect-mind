# The Anti-AI-Slop Manifesto

AI code generators are fundamentally biased toward verbosity. They want to explain what they did, boast about what they engineered, and decorate with emojis.

## The Axioms of Restraint

1. **Silence is Confidence**: The best software feels like it was designed by someone who doesn't need to shout to prove their competence.
2. **Deference to User Content**: The UI is a frame. The user's work is the art. Every extra word in a header steals pixels and attention from the user's canvas.
3. **Respect for Architecture**: Implementation details belong in commit messages and documentation, never on the screen.
